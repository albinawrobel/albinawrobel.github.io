{%- comment -%} Sorts everything into guides, articles and tools. Guides: posts with type: guide at the top. Articles: every other post. Tools: pages with tool: true at the top. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign guides = site.posts | where: "type", "guide" -%} {%- assign articles = site.posts | where_exp: "p", "p.type != 'guide'" -%} {%- assign tools = site.pages | where: "tool", true | sort: "title" -%}
