How it works

  • CAC (customer acquisition cost) = spend ÷ new customers.
  • Payback = how many months of gross profit from a customer it takes to earn back their CAC.
  • LTV (lifetime value) = monthly gross profit per customer ÷ monthly churn.
  • ROI = gross profit from these customers over your time window, minus spend, divided by spend. Churn is taken into account month by month.

Rules of thumb that many SaaS investors use: payback under 12 months and LTV:CAC of 3 or higher are healthy. These are starting points, not laws.
