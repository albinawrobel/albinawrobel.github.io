Is it a product problem or a GTM problem?

Not getting traction usually comes down to one of two things: the product isn't giving people a reason to stay, or the right people aren't finding it. Answer 10 quick questions to see which one you're most likely dealing with.

Each question is about what people actually do, not what they say, so answer with what's happened so far rather than what you hope will happen. It takes about two minutes, and everything runs in your browser; nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
